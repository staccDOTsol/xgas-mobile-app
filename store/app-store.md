## Description

xgas.dollars is a dollar-first earning app. Your balance is quoted in dollars, your yield is quoted and paid in dollars, and payouts leave in dollars through bank partners, X Money, or by simply staying put and continuing to earn.

**The boring door is the default.** Dollar Yield targets steady, dollar-quoted returns. Targets are not guarantees, and the app shows you the backing receipt for every dollar before you confirm anything.

**Spicier plays are double opt-in.** Riskier curve and counterparty plays are off by default, require two explicit opt-ins, and lead every quote with the worst case — max loss, printed first, in the biggest numbers we have.

**The 30% is printed, not buried.** Add dollars inside a native app and Apple/Google keep a 30% convenience fee — cuz app. The app shows you that cut, in plain numbers, before you pay, and shows you the web checkout price ($0 cut) right next to it. Same dollars, same yield.

No chain vocabulary in the app. Settlement metadata lives in receipts for anyone who wants it — and nowhere else.
