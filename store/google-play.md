## Full description

Dollars in. Dollars out. Yield paid in dollars.

xgas.dollars keeps one promise in plain sight: your balance is quoted in dollars, your earnings are quoted and paid in dollars, and your payouts leave in dollars — via bank partners (1–3 days), X Money to your handle (minutes), or by staying in and continuing to earn.

DEFAULT: DOLLAR YIELD
Nearly risk-free, dollar-quoted yield on the dollar. Targets are targets, not guarantees — every deposit shows its backing receipt before you confirm.

SPICIER PLAYS: DOUBLE OPT-IN
Riskier plays are off by default, need two explicit opt-ins, and print the worst case first on every quote. Nothing auto-executes: quote → review exact terms → you approve.

THE CONVENIENCE FEE, OUT LOUD
Onboard inside a native app and Apple/Google take 30% — a convenience fee, cuz app, paid to them, not to us. We print it before you pay and show the web checkout ($0 cut) beside it.

No chain vocabulary anywhere in the app. Settlement metadata lives in your receipts, and only there.
